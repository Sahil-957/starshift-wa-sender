1. Who we are

Starshift WA Sender ("the extension") is provided by {{BUSINESS_NAME}} ("we", "us"). You can reach us on WhatsApp at {{SUPPORT_MOBILE_LABEL}} or by email at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

2. What the extension does

The extension runs inside your own Chrome browser and automates the WhatsApp Web page that you are already logged in to. It reads your chat list so you can pick who to message, and it types and sends the messages you compose. It is not connected to WhatsApp's official Business API, and it never has access to your WhatsApp account outside your own browser.

3. What stays on your computer

All of the following is stored only in Chrome's local extension storage on your device. It is never sent to us, and we cannot read it:

  • Chat and group names, and any phone numbers, read from your WhatsApp Web session
  • Recipients you add, and any saved recipient lists
  • Message text, templates, placeholders and emoji
  • Files you attach, and their captions
  • Contacts imported from an Excel file or a Google Sheet
  • Campaign settings, schedules, progress and reports
  • Your unsubscribers list, chatbot rules and quick replies

Uninstalling the extension, or clearing its data from chrome://extensions, deletes all of it.

4. What our licence server receives

The extension talks to our server only to check that your subscription is valid. That server stores:

DataWhy
Your mobile numberIt is your account name
Your name (if your seller entered one)So we can identify your account for support
Your password, stored only as a salted hashTo log you in. We cannot read your actual password.
Your plan length and expiry dateTo know when your subscription ends

That is the complete list. Your contacts, your recipients, your message text and your attachments are never sent to this server.

5. Third parties

  • WhatsApp. Your messages go out through your own WhatsApp Web session, so WhatsApp handles them exactly as it handles messages you type by hand. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to them.
  • Google Sheets - only if you use it. When you paste a Google Sheets link, your browser fetches that sheet directly from Google. We never see the link or the sheet.
  • Translation. The Translate button uses Chrome's built-in on-device translator. Your message text does not leave your computer.
  • SMS. If you reset your password, a one-time code is sent to your mobile number through an SMS provider. Only your number and the code are shared with them.

6. What we never do

  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone for advertising
  • We do not read, store or forward the content of your WhatsApp messages
  • We do not upload your contact list anywhere
  • The extension contains no analytics, trackers or advertising code

8. How long we keep your account data

We keep your account record for as long as your subscription is active, and for a reasonable period afterwards so that renewing is easy. Ask us and we will delete it - write to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} from your registered number or email. Deleting your account ends your access to the extension.

9. Security

Passwords are stored only as salted scrypt hashes, never in plain text. Logins use a signed token that expires. We re-check your subscription on every use, so access can be withdrawn immediately. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you do not use anywhere else.
